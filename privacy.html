ProBeing — privacy

ProBeing is a personal activity tracker used by its owner only. Sign-ups are closed.

What it stores. The entries you log (work notes, prayers, M, money) are kept in the app's own Supabase database, readable only by your signed-in account.

Google data. If you connect Google, ProBeing reads the one Google Tasks list you choose, and (later) writes a copy of your entries to a spreadsheet it creates in your Google Drive. It asks for nothing else. The Google permission is stored encrypted on the server and is never sent to the browser.

Sharing. Nothing is sold or shared. Entry text may be sent to Google's Gemini API to name projects and write summaries.

Removing it. Settings → Google Tasks → Disconnect revokes the Google permission. You can also remove ProBeing at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
